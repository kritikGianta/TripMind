# Using TripMind

Help articles for the assistant. Policies (refunds, holds, promo codes, loyalty) are generated from the live configuration and are not repeated here.

## Searching for flights
Open Flights, choose where you are leaving from and going to, and pick dates. Results include direct flights and connections with one stop, with at least one hour to change planes at the same airport. Filter by stops, airline, departure time and price, and sort by best, cheapest, fastest or earliest. Times are local at each airport; a +1 means the flight lands the next day.

## Searching for stays
Open Stays, choose a city, dates, guests and rooms. Only rooms that fit your party are shown. The price covers every night and room of the stay. Filter by budget, free cancellation, breakfast, star rating, guest rating and facilities such as wheelchair access or a pool.

## Planning a whole trip
Open Plan a trip and give where you leave from, the destination and your dates. TripMind combines outbound and return flights with a stay for the nights in between and ranks complete trips. Each option shows why it ranks where it does and a Journey Confidence Report: connections, waiting time before check-in, the airport transfer, seats left and whether the room is refundable. Save or dismiss options; your choices are remembered. Book this trip holds both flights and the room in one checkout.

## Adding a stay or flight to a trip
Every booking belongs to a trip in My Trips. On a trip with only a flight, choose Add a stay; the city and dates are filled in and the new booking joins the same trip. A stay-only trip offers Add a flight in the same way.

## Finding and managing bookings
My Trips lists every trip. Open a trip, then Manage on a booking to see what is booked, the travellers, payments, refunds and the timeline of every change. From there you can cancel all or part of a booking and print the invoice and itinerary.

## Saved travellers and account
On the Account page you can save travellers you often book for. At checkout, choose a saved traveller instead of typing names. The Account page also shows your loyalty points and tier.

## Notifications
The bell in the top bar shows confirmations, cancellations, refunds and any change made by an airline or hotel. A simulated email copy is recorded for each notification; no real email is sent.

## What the AI does and does not do
The assistant answers from TripMind's own help and your trips. It can explain options and guide you to the right page, but it never books, pays, cancels or changes anything: you do that yourself. Your name, email and card details are never sent to AI providers, and contact details typed into a question are removed first. When no AI provider is available, TripMind answers with the most relevant help article instead.
